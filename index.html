WHO WE ARE

OtakuTV is Nigeria’s leading anime media and community platform — creating experiences where African otaku thrive. From nationwide conventions to cosplay showcases, we’ve united thousands of anime fans through content, culture, and connection.

Founded: 2021

Community Reach: 20,000+ fans across Nigeria

Flagship Event: Otaku Connect — the biggest anime convention in West Africa

Our Mission: To bridge African otaku culture with the global anime industry.

OUR CURRENT PROJECTS

🧳

Japan Anime Excursion

A 2-week cultural experience for African anime fans launching in 2026. We’re forming partnerships with tourism agencies in Japan.

🎟️

Otaku Tickets

OtakuVille

A physical hub in Nigeria featuring a themed café, gaming arena, and merchandise floor — Africa’s first otaku lifestyle center (2027-2030).
